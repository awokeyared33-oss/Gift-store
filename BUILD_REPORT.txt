  * High-fidelity product artwork rendered in fixed-size 115dp warm ivory container.

[3. MULTI-PRODUCT SELECTION & STEPPER CONTROLS]
- One-tap selection: Tapping an available product adds it to the sale (quantity = 1).
- Interactive Stepper: Selected cards show a minus/plus stepper (- Qty +).
- Tapping (+) increments quantity up to available in-stock count.
- Tapping (-) decrements quantity; if reduced to 0, item is cleanly removed from the cart.
- Multi-item support: Shop owner can select multiple different products in a single transaction.

[4. FIXED BOTTOM CHECKOUT PANEL]
- Positioned above the bottom navigation with luxury elevated card styling.
- Real-time cart tally:
  * "Selected items: X • Total quantity: Y"
  * "Total: XXX ETB" (Formatted via FinancialCalculator.formatBirr).
- Prominent [ CONFIRM SALE ] button in MakiEmerald (#0F4A3C) with gold accents.
- Disabled when cart is empty; shows spinner while processing.

[5. OPTIONAL CUSTOMER SELECTION]
- Non-intrusive "Customer: Walk-in (Optional)" pill in the checkout area.
- Tapping opens a customer selection modal to associate the sale with a registered customer.
- Clearing restores default walk-in mode without disrupting the current cart.

[6. ATOMIC TRANSACTION & RECEIPT]
- Review dialog confirms product lines, quantities, unit prices, subtotal, and assigned customer.
- Atomic Room transaction via MakiRepository.recordMultipleSales():
  * Deducts inventory stock for each product.
  * Creates immutable purchasePriceSnapshot and sellingPriceSnapshot records.
  * Logs SALE_DEDUCTION in inventory movements.
  * Records CRM previous gifts history if customer is assigned.
  * Atomic rollback if any product fails stock verification.
- Beautiful Sale Successful receipt dialog with Ethiopian calendar date, item breakdown,
  and Android Intent "Share Receipt" action.
